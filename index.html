My Education/Background/deal atm

I'm graduating from Fordham University in May 2026 with a Bachelor's in the Arts and Sciences for my majors in political science and history. I've written my thesis about the Catholic response to the 1957 Little Rock Crisis in Arkansas during the Black Freedom Movement in the United States by comparing the diocesan papers of the Little Rock Diocese with other diocesan papers from around the country. I spent a year and a half during my junior and senior year working on a manuscript as a research assistant for professors at Fordham and Case Western tracking the development of the content of the evangelical American electoral right-wing through archival work on publications like Jerry Falwell's Moral Majority Report. As of right now I have been accepted into graduate programs for journalism at Columbia University (Stabile Program for Investigative Journalism), and I'm deferring my intake into the master's program until August 2027 as I've been named a recipient of the Fulbright English Teaching Award with the U.S. State Department in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia from August 2026 to May 2027.
